Welcome to Sunny's

Drive Thru Shift VR puts you behind the counter of a roadside diner. Every car brings a new order, and it's up to you to cook it, build it and hand it through the window while the driver's patience ticks down.

Fast, correct orders earn tips. Spend them between shifts on new dishes, toppings and machines, and grow Sunny's from a burger stand into a full menu.

Cook it for real

Every station is hands-on. No menus, no button prompts: pick it up, cook it, stack it, serve it.

Work the grill

Flip patties and sausages and pull them before they burn. The meat warmer keeps them hot for the rush.

Squeeze the sauce

Ketchup, mustard, mayo and BBQ, drawn with the trigger. Squeeze harder for more, but too much gets messy.

Drop the fries

Fries, nuggets and onion rings in the basket, then into the pack and under the heat lamp.

Serve the window

Every driver has an order and a temper. Get it right and get it fast to keep the tips coming.

Bake and toast

Unlock the bakery corner for fresh cookies and toasted bagels with cream cheese.

Grow the diner

Start with burgers and fries. Unlock hot dogs, subs, shakes, coffee, desserts and more, one shift at a time.
